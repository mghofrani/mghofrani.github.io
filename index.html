About

I'm a postdoctoral research fellow in deep learning at the Nellie Georgiou-Karistianis Laboratory, Monash University. My PhD at Monash developed machine learning biomarkers of Huntington's disease progression from longitudinal brain MRI.

Before that I earned a Master's in Biomedical Engineering and a Bachelor's in Electrical Engineering. I've spent more than a decade on medical image analysis, EMG/EEG signal processing and computer vision, in both research and industry settings.
